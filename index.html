How to survive

Light is money.

Your lantern burns one coin every second it is on. Turn it off to conserve your purse, but you can only see your immediate surroundings. Explored paths leave a faint memory.

  • Gold circles: +8 coins. Each pile is collected once.
  • Violet star relic: optional treasure worth 25 points when carried through this chamber’s unlocked gate. It adds no coins. An unbanked relic is lost if you die.
  • Blue diamond: the chamber key.
  • Green gate: exit; requires the key.
  • Red spikes: lose 5 coins each time you enter.

The escape

Clear three freshly generated chambers: The Threshold offers wider light, The Crossroads adds more shortcuts, and The Last Passage narrows your light. On small screens, the view follows you with larger tiles. Spiked shortcuts trade 5 coins for a shorter route; explore a longer detour to avoid that spike. Coins carry over; keys reset. Entering chambers two and three grants 10 coins. You lose immediately at zero coins, even with the lantern off.

Finish with as many coins as you can. Final score = remaining coins + 20 per cleared chamber + 25 per secured relic. Failed runs also score cleared chambers. Relics are optional: all gates require only the key. Best scores for this scoring version are saved separately for each difficulty on this browser.

Explorer starts with 36 coins and a wide lantern. Standard starts with 24. Desperate starts with 18 and has a smaller lantern. There are no enemies or combat; route planning is the challenge.

Rules, fairness & accessibility

Each chamber is a connected 23 × 15 maze with spiked shortcuts and longer detours with impassable walls. Every key and gate is reachable. Coins and spikes never overlap the start, key, or gate. Walking into walls is free. Movement is limited to one tile every 130 milliseconds; holding a direction repeats. The lantern starts on in every chamber; its partial burn timer carries over. With the lantern off, time has no cost. Spikes remain dangerous on revisits.

Pausing or hiding this tab freezes movement and coin consumption. Resume explicitly when you return. Sound is optional and generated locally. Reduced-motion preferences disable the player pulse. Symbols distinguish objects in addition to color. The canvas requires spatial vision; this version does not offer a complete screen-reader mode.

No account, downloads, tracking, or external assets. Works offline after saving this HTML file. Best scores use local browser storage when available.
